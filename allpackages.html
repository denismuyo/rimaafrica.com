About our Packages

Discover unforgettable safari adventures crafted by local experts. Whether you're looking for a romantic escape, family adventure, or luxury wildlife tour, Rima Africa Safaris offers a variety of tailored packages to suit every traveler's dream. Explore Kenya's iconic destinations—from the vast plains of Maasai Mara to the shadow of Mount Kilimanjaro in Amboseli and the unique culture of Samburu.
